Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Overview

NextPop is a number-tapping game built by GlideNova. The short version: NextPop does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your scores, streaks, records, Daily times, settings and chosen theme are stored only on your iPhone. GlideNova never receives a copy. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Reset Progress, removes this data.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, your scores for Blitz, Daily and Memory are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is provided by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. GlideNova cannot see your Apple ID, email or other Game Center account details. Game Center is optional and the game works fully without it.

Purchases

NextPop Premium is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. GlideNova does not see or store your payment details.

No Tracking, No Ads

The NextPop app contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no third-party tracking. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

This Website

The glidenova.com website uses Google Analytics to count page visits. This applies to the website only, not to the NextPop app.

Children

NextPop is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to contact@glidenova.com.
