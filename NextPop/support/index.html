Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap the bubbles in order: 1, then 2, then 3. In Blitz you have 30 seconds and new numbers keep appearing. In Daily you clear 1 to 20 as fast as you can. In Memory, watch the order the bubbles light up and repeat it.

Why didn't my Daily count?

Only your first completed Daily each day (UTC) counts for your streak and the leaderboard. Later runs on the same day are practice and are labelled that way on the result card.

How do I turn the hint ring on or off?

Go to Settings and use “Hint next bubble.” It is on automatically for your first three games and then switches off, unless you choose otherwise.

What does Premium include?

A one-time $2.99 purchase that unlocks every past Daily board, Zen mode, unlimited Memory rounds, the Neon, Sorbet and Graphite themes, and progress charts. No subscription, no ads.

How do I restore Premium on a new device?

Open NextPop, go to Settings and tap “Restore Purchases.” Use the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

How do I see the leaderboards?

Open Stats. If you're not signed in to Game Center, tap “Sign in to Game Center,” then “Leaderboards.”

How do refunds work?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase.

Still need help?
