Frequently asked questions

How do I mark a photo as a favorite?

Click the heart icon on any thumbnail, or open the full-screen preview and press Space.

How do I open the full-screen preview?

Click anywhere on a thumbnail (not the heart icon). Use the ← and → arrow keys, or the on-screen arrows, to move through the set. Press Esc to close it.

How do I copy my favorites to a new folder?

Once you've marked at least one favorite, click "Copy Favorites…" in the toolbar and choose a destination. FotoFav creates a "Favorites" folder there and copies your originals into it — nothing is moved or altered.

What are categories, and how do I use them?

Categories are colored tags you create yourself. Hover a thumbnail and click the tag icon to assign one or more categories, or create a new one with its own color. Use the chips below the toolbar to filter the grid down to just one category at a time.

Do my favorites and categories survive closing the app?

Categories and which photos they're assigned to are saved automatically and will still be there next time you open the same folder. Favorites are intentionally session-only — the idea is you mark them and copy them out right away.

Does FotoFav send my photos anywhere?

No. FotoFav has no network access at all — everything happens locally on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which macOS versions does FotoFav support?

FotoFav runs on macOS 12 (Monterey) and later, on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
