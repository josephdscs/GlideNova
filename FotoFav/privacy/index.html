Privacy Policy

Effective: August 19, 2026

FotoFav is made by GlideNova. It's a local macOS app for browsing a folder of photos, marking favorites, and copying them to a new folder. This policy explains, plainly, what happens to your data — which is almost nothing.

The short version: FotoFav has no server, no analytics, no account system, and makes no network requests of any kind. Everything described below happens entirely on your own Mac.

What FotoFav accesses

When you choose a folder or a destination through the standard macOS file picker, FotoFav reads the image files in that folder and writes copies to the destination you chose. It never accesses any other files or folders on your Mac.

What FotoFav stores

Two small things, saved locally in ~/Library/Application Support/PhotoFavorites on your own Mac:

  • The names and colors of any categories you create
  • Which category tags are assigned to which photo file paths

Favorites you mark are kept only in memory for your current session and are not saved to disk — they exist so you can copy them out, and clear once you close the app or open a new folder.

What FotoFav does not do

  • It does not upload, sync, or transmit your photos anywhere
  • It does not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage data
  • It does not require or create an account
  • It does not include third-party SDKs or trackers

Children

FotoFav is rated 4+ on the App Store. The app does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else, as we do not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@glidenova.app.
