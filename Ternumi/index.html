How it works

Pair once. Then just send.

1

Sign in with Apple

Only your first name is shared with your partner. We don't ask for your email.

2

Pair with a 6-digit code

One of you gets a code and sends it as a link. The other types it in or taps the link. The code lasts 48 hours.

3

Add the widget

Put Ternumi on your home screen or lock screen. Every note or doodle your partner sends replaces the last one.

Features

Small, warm, and always within reach.

📝

Notes

Up to 280 characters on a coloured card. Good morning, a joke, what's for dinner.

✏️

Doodles

Draw with your finger and send it. Hearts, stick figures, whatever you'd scribble on a sticky note.

📱

Home and lock screen widget

New notes arrive by notification and the widget updates right away, even if the app is closed.

💬

A question a day

You both get the same question. Your partner's answer is revealed once you've both answered. Keep the streak going.

📚

History

Every note and doodle you've exchanged, in one scroll. Always free.

🔒

Private by default

No ads, no third-party tracking, no selling data. Your notes are sent over a secure connection and stored in the EU. Only the sender's name travels in the notification; the app fetches the note itself.

Free to start. One subscription for both of you.

Each of you can send 3 notes or doodles for free. Receiving, the widget, your history and the daily question are always free. Ternumi Plus removes the limit and adds more colours and brushes, and one subscription unlocks it for both partners while you're paired.
