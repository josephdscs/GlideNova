Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap an arrow and it slides off the board, but only if nothing is in its way. If it's blocked it bumps back. Clear the arrows in the right order.

What is Rush?

Endless mode. Every three clears a new wave of arrows drops in. A blocked tap costs a heart; the run ends when you're out of hearts or the board is full. Every board can always be cleared: the challenge is your choices.

What is the Daily?

One board per day, the same for everyone (UTC). Clear it as fast as you can; each mistake adds 5 seconds. Your first clear each day counts for your streak and the leaderboard.

What does Premium include?

A one-time $2.99 purchase: a free continue in every Rush run, replays of the last 30 Daily boards, and the Ember and Ocean themes. No subscription.

How do I restore Premium on a new device?

Open Clearway, go to Settings and tap “Restore Purchases.” Use the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

How do refunds work?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase.
