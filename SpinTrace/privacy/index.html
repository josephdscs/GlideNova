Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Overview

Spin Peek is a drawing activity for children, built by GlideNova. The short version: Spin Peek does not collect any personal data from anyone, children or adults. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app does not connect to any GlideNova server.

Data Stored on Your Device

Finished discs, Daily streaks, challenge stickers, settings, the chosen colour theme and any pictures drawn with Make your own are stored only on the device. GlideNova never receives a copy. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Reset progress, removes this data.

Printing and Sharing

When you print a disc or save it as a PDF or SVG file, the file is created on the device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (a printer, Files, Mail) is your choice and is handled by iOS, not by GlideNova.

Purchases

Spin Peek Premium is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. GlideNova does not see or store any payment details. Purchases, and links that leave the app, are behind a grown-ups check so a child cannot reach them alone.

Children

Spin Peek is designed for children and collects no personal information. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to review, export or delete on our side. Parents with questions can write to the address below.

This Website

The glidenova.com website uses Google Analytics to count page visits. This applies to the website only, not to the Spin Peek app.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. If a future version ever collects data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to contact@glidenova.com.
