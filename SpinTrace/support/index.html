Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Turn the disc until the arrow points at number 1, then trace the glowing slot with your finger. Turn to number 2 and trace again. Keep going: after the last slot, the mystery picture appears.

My child finds it hard. Can it help?

Yes. The next slot always glows, and Auto-trace draws a step for you. Start with Easy discs (fewer steps) and move up to Tricky ones.

What is the Daily?

A new mystery disc every day, free. Finish it on its day to grow your streak. Earlier days can be played with Premium.

How do I print a disc?

Open a disc and tap the printer icon. Pick the size and paper, then print or save the PDF. Each slot has a small number badge where tracing starts. Cut out the disc, pin it through the centre mark onto paper, turn it to rim number 1 and trace slot 1.

What does Premium include?

A one-time $3.99 purchase: every library disc, every past Daily, Make your own, SVG export for Cricut and laser cutters, four extra colour themes and four extra crayons. No subscription.

Why is there a maths question before buying?

It is a grown-ups check, so children cannot buy anything or leave the app on their own. Type the answer and tap OK.

How do I restore Premium on a new device?

Open Spin Peek, go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases". Use the same Apple ID that made the purchase.

How do refunds work?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase.
